# Insight note

## Source type

Public source / anonymised interview synthesis / survey aggregate / usability test

## Observation

What was actually observed? Keep this separate from interpretation.

## Confidence / limitations

Sample size, bias, ambiguity, or other caveats.

## Product hypothesis

What might this imply for the product?

## Smallest test

What is the cheapest way to test the hypothesis without committing to a large feature?

## Decision

Adopt / test / defer / reject

> Never paste raw identifiable participant material into this template.
