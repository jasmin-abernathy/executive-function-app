# Complementary tools finding — 2026-08-14

## Observed signal

In the analysed public/user-research corpus, **14 of 21 testimonies that used at least one organisation app also used at least one tool from another category** (67% in that observed sample).

The most recurrent complementary category was timers / alarms / persistent reminders.

## Product implication

Do not assume that success means replacing every tool. The app should primarily make the **links between capture, starting, noticing time, interruption and return** easier.

This supports:

- keeping the core narrow;
- a strong timer/focus component;
- ultra-fast capture;
- future integrations/hand-offs where useful;
- avoiding a forced “all-in-one” design.

## Limitation

This is an observed corpus signal, not a prevalence estimate for all people with ADHD. It must not be presented as a representative population statistic.
