RésoSoin

An independent foundation for medical practices: their own website, appointments, explicit automation and interoperability with existing tools, without imposed AI.

Current stage
Sandbox prototype · practice pilot pending
Platform
Web · Healthcare
Reviewed
5 November 2026

What it does and what is planned

  • Surveys for health professionals and patients
  • Resumable drafts and early aggregated findings
  • Independent practice websites, bookings and automation under study

Latest development

Flutter patient and practitioner prototypes, appointment management and a tested SQLite sandbox are available for development. A practice pilot has not yet launched.

Known limitations and next steps

  • These are development prototypes, not a deployed medical service.
  • Integrations and regulatory requirements still need real-world validation.
