VeVak

Share a location by SMS with explicitly authorised contacts, without requiring a cloud account or Internet access for the core workflow.

Current stage
Android beta · testing distribution delayed
Platform
Android · FOSS
Reviewed
5 November 2026

What it does and what is planned

  • SMS location sharing with explicitly authorised contacts
  • Core workflow without cloud account or Internet connection
  • Permission, notification and anti-tracking safeguards

Latest development

Android fixes and long-term APK signing are being prepared. Distribution to the tester panel remains delayed until installation and security checks are completed.

Known limitations and next steps

  • Tester invitations are delayed pending reliable signed builds and checks on real phones. Google Play closed-test requirements also apply to some developer accounts.
  • Location sharing still requires careful configuration of trusted contacts.
