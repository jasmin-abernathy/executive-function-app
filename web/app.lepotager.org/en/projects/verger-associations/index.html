What is Verger Associations for?

Meetings, group decisions, tasks, events and volunteers are often managed across several unrelated documents. Verger Associations aims to bring them together in one open-source app, without requiring a new messaging or accounting platform.

It targets associations, community groups and cooperatives, with local storage and portable exports.

Example: a committee meeting

  1. Prepare the agenda.
  2. Collect a proposal and objections.
  3. Record the decision and review date.
  4. Assign a follow-up action.
  5. Export the meeting record to the association's archives.

This workflow exists in the prototype but needs field testing with real associations.

Current development stage

The PWA includes members, meetings, decisions, tasks, events, volunteer requests and JSON/Markdown exports. Onboarding, mutual aid and consultations have initial implementations. An older Webxdc prototype served as a proof of concept.

Offline and reconnect testing on real devices remains pending. The optional sync server still uses a shared token: it cannot yet securely host unrelated associations.

What's next and what's out of scope

  • Run a pilot with 4–7 participants and at least two real meetings.
  • Add proper accounts, invitations, permissions and revocation.
  • Build and test the Capacitor Android version; no public-ready APK is currently announced.
  • Consider reporting, alerting and optional integrations only after pilot feedback.

This is not yet an accounting, official records or messaging system. Avoid sensitive production data until security validation.
