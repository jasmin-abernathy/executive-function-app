Dendrila Monitor

A companion to Dendrila Privacy: keeping local scan history while building a multi-site overview for Studio and Agency teams.

Current stage
Local and multi-site prototype PRs · not sold
Platform
WordPress · module complémentaire à Dendrila Privacy
Reviewed
11 October 2026

Features being developed

  • Local history and comparisons between scans of similar coverage, without storing full HTML pages.
  • Notices new third-party services, scan errors and changes to consent blocking.
  • Optional local alerts and WordPress cron-health checks.
  • Studio/Agency prototype: signed HTTPS linking, multi-client dashboard and CSV export.

Current limitations

Local monitoring is intended to stay free with Dendrila Privacy: no paid Solo plan. Studio and Agency are not sold yet; tests on multiple real WordPress sites, hosting and integration are pending. Advanced Agency features are unfinished.

Follow development

The repository is private while integration and pilot tests continue.
