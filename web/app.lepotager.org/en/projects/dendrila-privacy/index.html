Dendrila Privacy

A free WordPress plugin for analysing trackers and third-party services, documenting privacy practices and optionally managing consent. Studio and Agency are multi-site tools still in development.

Current stage
Version 0.0.5 · published
Platform
WordPress · GPL
Reviewed

Versions and pricing

The plugin remains free, without artificial restrictions. Local monitoring and alerts will also remain free; automation is still in development. Paid plans focus only on coordinating multiple sites and clients.

Studio and Agency prices are provisional estimates, not final prices. No paid subscription is available yet.

Audit and document your website without payment or mandatory commercial monitoring.
