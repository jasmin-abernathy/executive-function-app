Autonomy

Executive functions / ADHD

A non-punitive app for capturing, choosing a next action, starting, handling interruption and returning without turning absence into failure.

Current stage
Android prototype · user testing
Platform
Android · AGPL
Reviewed
9 October 2026

What it does and what is planned

  • Quick task and note capture, then next-action selection
  • Focus timer, interruption handling and non-punitive resumption
  • Optional ten-sided die, task ordering, local data and FR/EN UI

Latest development

P1 workflow works; V2 study and topic-based public results with small-group privacy safeguards.

Known limitations and next steps

  • Onboarding, user experience and visual identity are still being refined.
  • The prototype does not claim clinically validated effectiveness.
