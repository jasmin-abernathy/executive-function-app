What it does and what is planned

  • Work-based search with progressive edition grouping
  • French and English ebook sources, including BnF and Gallica
  • Independent bookshop discovery around Metz and Moselle

Latest development

On 9 October, federated searches, bookshop/ebook results and resource loading were improved.

Known limitations and next steps

  • Search remains in alpha: grouping and coverage still need work.
  • No integrated payment or unified multi-bookshop checkout yet.
