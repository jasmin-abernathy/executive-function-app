Communication libre

A browser-first route to self-hostable collective messaging, with encryption where the chosen upstream provides it and no mandatory app for the pilot.

Current stage
Labs · device testing pending
Platform
Web · free messaging
Reviewed
9 October 2026

What it does and what is planned

  • Browser-first collective messaging
  • Self-hosting labs for Databag and Tuwunel with Cinny
  • Separate configuration and administration secrets

Latest development

Databag prioritised; browser notification limitations documented.

Known limitations and next steps

  • No unified public service is available yet.
  • Mobile notifications and phone experience still need real-world testing.
