WordPress

Dendrila Age Assurance

A privacy-oriented age-assurance foundation for WordPress, designed to minimise exchanged data and gate selected content before rendering.

Current stage
Foundation in PR · not released
Platform
WordPress · PHP 7.4+ · prototype
Reviewed
11 October 2026

Features being developed

  • Sitewide or selected-page protection before the theme renders.
  • Server-side AgeVerif OAuth2 adapter with a short-lived signed local proof.
  • Designed not to persist identity documents, country or provider access tokens.
  • Coverage for WordPress pages, REST responses, archives, feeds and sitemaps.

Current limitations

Images and files already directly accessible by URL or CDN may bypass the PHP gate. Direct-media protection remains a critical limitation. No regulatory approval or compliance claim. Development is on an unmerged PR.

Follow development

The repository is private while integration and pilot tests continue.
