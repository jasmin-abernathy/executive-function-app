Delivery aggregator

A prototype for comparing delivery services without collecting platform passwords or bypassing their official checkout.

Current stage
Comparator design · not launched
Platform
Web · AGPL
Reviewed
9 October 2026

What it does and what is planned

  • Compare real observed prices on delivery platforms
  • Keep observations locally without collecting platform credentials
  • Compare equivalent baskets and delivery conditions

Latest development

Target flow defined: recent manual observations, local storage and like-for-like basket comparison.

Known limitations and next steps

  • No complete public comparator is available yet.
  • Automated data access would require platform authorisation.
