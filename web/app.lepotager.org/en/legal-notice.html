04

Personal data

app.lepotager.org hosts project pages, prototypes, user-research questionnaires and, when requested by the user, functions for receiving a reminder or joining a research panel.

Questionnaires and studies

Questionnaire answers are used to understand behaviours, difficulties and needs related to the projects presented on this site. Where a questionnaire can be resumed later, the technical information needed for that resumption is handled separately from the results used for research.

Email reminders

When an adult voluntarily schedules a reminder, their email address, message language and the information needed to resume the questionnaire are used only to send the requested reminder. These data are stored separately from questionnaire answers, and the reminder record is deleted after it is sent or cancelled.

Research panel

Joining the research panel is independent from completing a questionnaire. An email address is added to the panel only after confirmation. It is used only to invite the person to future research, studies or user tests related to Le Potager des Apps projects. It is kept until the person unsubscribes or the panel is closed and is not used for a general commercial newsletter.

Access and rights

Data are accessible to Jasmin Lévêque as part of Le Potager du Web. o2switch acts as the technical hosting and email provider. You may request access to, correction, deletion or restriction of your personal data, and object to processing where that right applies, by emailing contact@lepotager.org. You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority (CNIL).
