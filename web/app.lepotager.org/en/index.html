Approach

Keep projects small long enough to keep them understandable.

Le Verger is used to test concrete answers before piling on features. Projects may evolve, merge or stop: what matters is documenting the need, the limits and the real maturity level.

01

Start with use

A project starts with a concrete problem or observed friction. The feature list comes afterwards.

02

Keep control

Local processing, controlled hosting, minimal permissions and limited dependencies are preferred when they make sense.

03

Stay reversible

Exportable formats, open code where possible and bridges to existing tools instead of creating another lock-in.

04

Say where we are

Prototype, beta, pilot or early idea: the displayed status should describe the project as it exists today.
