# Research and evidence policy

## What this project can say now

The product can be described as **co-designed and informed by user research and available recommendations**.

It must not be described as clinically validated, therapeutic or proven to treat ADHD unless future evidence actually supports such claims.

## Repository boundary

Allowed under `research/`:

- aggregated findings;
- anonymised synthesis;
- public-source notes;
- blank research templates;
- documented product decisions derived from research.

Not allowed:

- raw survey exports;
- email addresses / recontact lists;
- identifiable transcripts;
- screenshots of private communities;
- participant IDs that can be re-linked to answers.

## Product analytics are not a substitute for research

The app should not introduce behavioural tracking simply because user research is useful. Beta studies can use explicit, separate protocols and participant consent.
